Contact Guidelines

If you have a question, feel free to ask.

If you have a bug to report, please make sure to include screenshots and steps to recreate the issue in your message. Without this information, it's hard to recreate and diagnose the issue.

If you have a suggestion, please go ahead and send it! I'm looking for new things to add every day, and any input or ideas are appreciated.


How to Contact Us

The most preferred way for you to contact us would be on Discord. It's the place that we're checking most, so if you have to contact us for any reason, this is the best place for you to do it. You can join the Drap's Labs Discord server here, or you can message us directly. The Staff links on the About page are also clickable, and will open our profile in a new window. However, if for whatever reason you are unable or don't want to contact me via discord, I am also reachable through email.

Please Note: An email may have a slower response times. While I do check my email often, sometimes messages end up in spam.

You can reach me by emailing this address: drapadon@drapadon.org.
